Clips to hear

The audio script played every clip to a speech-to-text model and compared what it heard with what the clip should say. These are the clips where the two differed, the word clips much longer than the rest, and any clip said again at your request. A flag only means "worth a listen": a lone short word is easy to mishear, and in Latin American Spanish some words sound exactly like others (haber and a ver, vez and ves), which the model cannot tell apart.

Press Play, then choose Sounds right or Say it again. Your choices are kept in this browser, so you can stop and come back.
