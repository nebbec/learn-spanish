title: Every noun is el or la

In Spanish, every noun (a word for a person or a thing) is either masculine or feminine.
Masculine words use el for the and un for a, like el hermano and un amigo.
Feminine words use la for the and una for a, like la hermana and una amiga.
This is true for things too, so coffee is masculine: el café.
Words ending in -o are usually el and words ending in -a are usually la, but not always, so learn el or la together with each new word.

example: Tengo un hermano y una hermana. | I have a brother and a sister.
example: La madre de Ana está aquí. | Ana's mother is here.
example: Quiero un café, por favor. | I'd like a coffee, please.
