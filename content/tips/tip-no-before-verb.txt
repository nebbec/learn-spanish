title: No goes before the verb

To say not in Spanish, you just put no right before the action word, the verb.
So sé means I know, and no sé means I don't know.
Spanish has no extra word like don't, so no entiendo means I don't understand.
When you answer a question with no, you often say it twice: No, no sé means No, I don't know.
The first no answers the question, and the second no goes before the verb.

example: No, no sé. | No, I don't know.
example: No quiero café. | I don't want coffee.
example: Él no está aquí. | He isn't here.
