title: Tú and usted

Spanish has two ways to say you.
Use tú with friends, family and people your age, and usted to be polite with strangers and older people.
With usted, the verb takes the same form as for he or she, so you say puede, not puedes.
¿Puedes ayudarme? asks a friend for help, and ¿Puede ayudarme? asks a stranger politely.
If you're not sure which one to use, usted is the safe choice.

example: Perdón, ¿puede ayudarme? | Excuse me, can you help me?
example: Ana, ¿puedes ayudarme? | Ana, can you help me?
example: Buenos días, ¿cómo está usted? | Good morning, how are you?
