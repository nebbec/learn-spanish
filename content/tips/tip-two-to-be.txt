title: Two verbs for "to be"

Spanish has two verbs that both mean to be: ser and estar.
Use ser (soy, eres, es) for what someone is: their name, where they're from, their job, what they're like.
Use estar (estoy, estás, está) for how someone is right now and where they are.
So soy de México tells people where you come from, but estoy bien tells them how you feel today.
If it's about how you feel or where you are at the moment, reach for estar.

example: Soy Ana y estoy muy bien. | I'm Ana and I'm very well.
example: Él es de Chile y está aquí. | He's from Chile and he's here.
example: Ella está en Lima. | She's in Lima.
