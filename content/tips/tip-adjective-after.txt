title: Describing words come after and match

In Spanish, a describing word usually comes after the thing it describes, so a big coffee is un café grande.
It also matches that thing: use the -o ending for an el word and the -a ending for a la word, as in un amigo nuevo and una amiga nueva.
When there is more than one, add -s to both words, as in dos amigos nuevos.
Words that end in -e, like grande, are the same for el words and la words.
So say the thing first, then make the describing word's ending match it.

example: Quiero un café grande. | I want a big coffee.
example: Mi hermana tiene una amiga nueva. | My sister has a new friend.
example: Tengo dos hermanos pequeños. | I have two little brothers.
