title: Questions and ¿ ¡

In Spanish, a question opens with an upside-down ¿ and closes with ?, so you know from the start that it's a question.
Exclamations work the same way, with ¡ at the start and ! at the end, as in ¡Hola!
For a yes-or-no question, you can take a normal sentence and raise your voice at the end: eres de México means you are from Mexico, and ¿Eres de México? asks it.
Question words always carry an accent mark, like qué, dónde and cómo.
The accent doesn't change the sound much, but it marks the word as a question word, so keep it when you ask.

example: ¿Eres de México? | Are you from Mexico?
example: ¿Dónde está tu hermana? | Where is your sister?
example: ¡Muy bien, gracias! | Very well, thanks!
