title: Regular -ar, -er and -ir verbs

Most Spanish verbs follow one of three patterns, depending on whether they end in -ar, -er or -ir.
To use one, drop that ending and add a new one that shows who is doing it.
For I, add -o: hablo means I speak, como means I eat, vivo means I live.
For you, add -as to -ar verbs and -es to the others: hablas, comes, vives.
For he or she, add -a to -ar verbs and -e to the others: habla, come, vive.

example: Trabajo en un hotel. | I work in a hotel.
example: ¿Dónde vives? | Where do you live?
example: Mi madre come aquí. | My mother eats here.
