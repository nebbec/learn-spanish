title: Por and para

Por and para can both mean for, but they point in different directions.
Para looks ahead to a goal or to the person who gets something: para mi madre means for my mother, and para comer means in order to eat.
Para can also give a deadline, so para mañana means by tomorrow.
Por looks back at a reason or an exchange: you already know por favor, and gracias por todo means thanks for everything.
Por can also mean through or around a place, so por aquí means around here.

example: Este café es para mi madre. | This coffee is for my mother.
example: Gracias por la ayuda. | Thanks for the help.
example: ¿Hay un baño por aquí? | Is there a bathroom around here?
