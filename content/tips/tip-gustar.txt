title: Gustar works backwards

Spanish says I like backwards: me gusta el café really means the coffee pleases me.
The thing you like does the pleasing, so the verb matches that thing, not you.
Say me gusta for one thing and me gustan for two or more things.
To ask someone if they like something, swap me for te: ¿te gusta el café? means do you like coffee?

example: Me gusta mucho el café. | I really like coffee.
example: Me gustan los hoteles pequeños. | I like small hotels.
example: ¿Te gusta la cerveza? | Do you like beer?
