title: Me, te, lo go before the verb

The small words for me, you, him, her and it are me, te, lo and la, and they go right before the verb.
So I love you is te quiero, which word for word is you I love.
Lo veo means I see him or I see it, and la veo means I see her.
When the verb is in its dictionary form, the one ending in -ar, -er or -ir, the small word attaches to the end of it instead.
So to help me is ayudarme, all one word.

example: Te quiero mucho, mi amor. | I love you so much, my love.
example: Lo veo mañana. | I'll see him tomorrow.
example: ¿Puedes llamarme mañana? | Can you call me tomorrow?
