title: Going to: voy a…

To talk about plans, Spanish uses ir a, just like going to in English.
Take the right form of ir (voy, vas or va), add a, then add a second verb.
That second verb stays in its basic form, the one ending in -ar, -er or -ir that you'd find in a dictionary, like hablar or entender.
So voy a hablar means I'm going to speak, and ¿vas a venir? means are you going to come?
Add hoy or mañana to say when your plan happens.

example: Voy a hablar inglés. | I'm going to speak English.
example: ¿Vas a estar en casa hoy? | Are you going to be home today?
example: Mi amiga va a necesitar ayuda mañana. | My friend is going to need help tomorrow.
