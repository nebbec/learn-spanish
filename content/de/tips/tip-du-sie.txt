title: Du and Sie

German has two words for you: du for friends, family, children and people your age, and Sie for strangers, shop staff and officials.
The polite Sie always has a capital S, even in the middle of a sentence.
With Sie, the verb takes its dictionary form, just as it does with wir (we) and sie (they): Können Sie mir helfen? means can you help me?
Sein (to be) is the exception, because you are is Sie sind.
Ihr means you when you talk to several friends at once, but this app leaves its forms out.

example: Entschuldigung, können Sie mir helfen? | Excuse me, can you help me?
example: Sind Sie aus Berlin? | Are you from Berlin?
example: Kannst du mir helfen? | Can you help me? (to a friend)
