title: Liking: gern and mögen

To say you like doing something, use the normal verb and put gern right after it: Ich trinke gern Kaffee means I like drinking coffee.
To say you don't like doing it, put nicht before gern: Ich arbeite nicht gern means I don't like working.
To like a thing or a person, use mögen, which is ich mag and du magst: Ich mag Musik means I like music.
Ich möchte comes from mögen too, and it's the polite I would like, which is perfect for asking for things.

example: Ich lerne gern Deutsch. | I like learning German.
example: Er arbeitet nicht gern. | He doesn't like working.
example: Magst du Musik? | Do you like music?
