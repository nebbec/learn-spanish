title: German always says who

In casual English you can sometimes skip the I, but German always says who is doing something.
Bin on its own is not a sentence, but ich bin means I am.
That is why the cards teach the person and the verb together: ich bin, du bist, er ist.
Learn each pair as one chunk, and never leave out the ich, du or er when you speak.

example: Ich bin Anna. | I'm Anna.
example: Du bist aus London. | You're from London.
example: Er ist Max und sie ist Lena. | He's Max and she's Lena.
