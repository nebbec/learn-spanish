title: Plurals have no single rule

English mostly adds -s for more than one, but German plurals come in several patterns.
Das Kind becomes die Kinder, die Frau becomes die Frauen, der Bruder becomes die Brüder, and das Hotel becomes die Hotels.
Whether the noun is der, die or das, the word for the in the plural is always die.
There's no rule you can rely on, so learn each plural together with its noun, and the answer on each card shows it to you.
When you order, drinks often stay singular, so you say zwei Kaffee, bitte.

example: Ich habe zwei Brüder. | I have two brothers.
example: Die Kinder trinken Wasser. | The children are drinking water.
example: Zwei Kaffee, bitte. | Two coffees, please.
