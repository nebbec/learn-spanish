title: Talking about the past: the Perfekt

To talk about the past in conversation, German uses haben or sein plus a past form of the verb, and that past form goes at the end.
Ich habe gearbeitet means I worked or I have worked, and ich bin nach Hause gegangen means I went home.
Most verbs use haben, but verbs of moving or changing, like gehen, kommen and aufstehen, use sein.
You don't have to guess which one: each verb's answer shows its past with the right helper, like hat gemacht or ist gegangen.

example: Ich habe heute gearbeitet. | I worked today.
example: Wir haben Kaffee getrunken. | We drank coffee.
example: Er ist nach Hause gegangen. | He went home.
