title: The verb comes second

In a German statement, the verb always comes second.
Second means the second part of the sentence, and a part can be one word like ich or a group of words like der Mann.
You can start with the person, as in Ich gehe heute nach Hause, which means I'm going home today.
If you start with something else, like a time or a place, the verb still comes second and the person moves behind it: Heute gehe ich nach Hause.
So when you start with heute, morgen, jetzt or hier, put the verb next, then the person.

example: Heute gehe ich nach Hause. | Today I'm going home.
example: Morgen geht Anna nach Berlin. | Tomorrow Anna is going to Berlin.
example: Hier ist das Hotel. | The hotel is here.
