title: Questions start with the verb

To ask a yes-or-no question, put the verb first: du bist müde means you are tired, and Bist du müde? means are you tired?
English often adds do (do you have water?), but German simply moves the verb to the front: Hast du Wasser?
With a question word like was, wer, wo, wann, wie or warum, the question word comes first and the verb comes straight after it.
So Wo ist das Hotel? means where is the hotel, in the same order as English.

example: Hast du einen Bruder? | Do you have a brother?
example: Wo ist das Hotel? | Where is the hotel?
example: Warum ist sie müde? | Why is she tired?
