title: Every noun is der, die or das

Every German noun is masculine, feminine or neuter, and the word for the shows which one: der, die or das.
This applies to people and things alike: der Vater, die Mutter, das Haus, and even das Kind.
You often can't guess it, so learn every noun with its der, die or das as part of the word.
If you get the word right but use the wrong der, die or das, it counts as Nearly.
Every noun also starts with a capital letter, wherever it is in the sentence.

example: Der Mann möchte Kaffee. | The man would like coffee.
example: Die Frau ist aus Berlin. | The woman is from Berlin.
example: Das Kind ist sehr müde. | The child is very tired.
