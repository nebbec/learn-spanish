# Learning path

The order `npm run deck` computed from the tags (rules in docs/design.md, "Order is computed, not hand-written"). Rewritten by every build: edit `content/units.json` or the tags, not this file.

A card marked *not in the deck* keeps its place in the order and the count, and is left out of `content/deck.json` for the reason given.

## Unit 1 · Me and you (`me-and-you`)

Now you can say I, you, he, she, it and we, say yes or no, and say please and thank you.

1. `ich-i` · [I] am tired → ich · *example uses words not met yet: bin, heute, total, müde*

## Frequency phase

45. `sein-be` · to be → sein · *example uses words not met yet: heute, total, müde*
46. `haben-have` · to have → haben · *example uses words not met yet: heute, Abend, Zeit*
47. `werden-become` · to become → werden
48. `ihr-you-plural` · are [you] guys coming tonight? (several friends) → ihr · *example uses words not met yet: Kommt, heute, Abend*
   - Why: Use ihr for several friends at once; du for one friend, Sie for strangers.
49. `der-who-that` · the man [who] lives here (short word, not welcher) → der
50. `zu-closed` · closed (short everyday word) → zu
51. `sein-his` · That's [his] dog → sein
   - Why: Use sein for his (or its); for her, use ihr.
52. `sie-they` · [They] are at home. → sie
   - Why: Sie is both she and they; the verb tells you which: sie ist is she is, sie sind is they are.
53. `sie-her` · I know [her] well. → sie
   - Why: Use sie for her as the object (I know her); before a noun, her is ihr (ihr Bruder).
54. `ein-one` · Just [one] beer, please (before a noun, not counting) → ein · *not in the deck: waiting for your decision*
   - Why: Use ein for one before a noun; say eins when counting on its own.
55. `werden-will` · We [will] see (future helper verb) → werden · *example uses words not met yet: dich, morgen, anrufen*
   - Why: Use werden for will (the future); ich will means I want, from wollen.
56. `zu-too` · That's [too] expensive → zu
57. `ja-particle-really` · That's [really] great! (particle of surprise, not wirklich) → ja · *not in the deck: waiting for your decision*
58. `ihr-her` · [her] brother is here → ihr
   - Why: Use ihr for things that belong to her; sein for things that belong to him.
59. `ihr-their` · [their] dog is huge → ihr · *example uses words not met yet: verkaufen, Auto, Anna, Tom*
