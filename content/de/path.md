# Learning path

The order `npm run deck` computed from the tags (rules in docs/design.md, "Order is computed, not hand-written"). Rewritten by every build: edit `content/units.json` or the tags, not this file.

A card marked *not in the deck* keeps its place in the order and the count, and is left out of `content/deck.json` for the reason given.

## Unit 1 · Me and you (`me-and-you`)

Now you can say I, you, he, she, it and we, say yes or no, and say please and thank you.

1. `ich-i` · [I] am tired → ich
2. `sie-she` · [She] is my sister. → sie · *example uses words not met yet: Ist, deine, Schwester*
3. `du-you` · Are [you] tired? (one person, informal) → du · *example uses words not met yet: bist, mein, bester, Freund*
4. `es-it` · [it]'s raining → es · *example uses words not met yet: regnet, schon, wieder*
5. `wir-we` · [we] are going home → wir · *example uses words not met yet: gehen, heute, ins, Kino*
6. `er-he` · [He] is my brother. → er
7. `ja-yes` · yes (the everyday answer) → ja
8. `nein-no` · no (the answer, opposite of yes) → nein · *example uses words not met yet: habe, keine, Zeit*
9. `bitte-please` · please → bitte

12. `phrase-no-thank-you` · No, thank you. → Nein, danke. · *example uses words not met yet: Möchtest, einen*
